Planning point

Best uses for publisher dice

Custom logo dice fit campaign launches, setting books, convention bundles, deluxe editions, direct-store merchandise, and limited rewards for community supporters.

Planning point

Symbol design rules

Original symbols work best when they are bold, simple, and readable at dice-face size. Thin lines, tiny letters, and complex seals can lose clarity after engraving, filling, or polishing.

Planning point

Recommended dice types

Custom D20 faces are common for publisher logos and faction marks. Full 7-piece sets work well for deluxe products. Metal dice can support premium editions, while resin dice can match setting colors and character themes.

Planning point

Approval process

Plan a sample review for face position, color, number fill, readability, packaging fit, and whether the dice match the tone of the RPG product line.

FAQ

Common buyer questions.

Can RPG publishers make custom logo D20 dice?

Yes. Send original vector artwork, target face placement, material preference, number fill color, packaging format, quantity, and launch date.

Can you make dice based on an RPG setting?

Yes, if the symbols and visual direction are original or supplied by the rights holder. We can develop colors and themes around factions, guilds, magic schools, classes, monsters, and locations.

What makes a good custom dice symbol?

A good dice symbol is bold, simple, readable at small size, and easy to engrave or fill cleanly. Avoid tiny text and very thin details.
