Direct answer

What are elemental dice sets?

Elemental dice sets are polyhedral RPG dice designed around recognizable fantasy forces. Fire, frost, storm, poison, radiant, necrotic, shadow, and arcane themes use coordinated color, transparency, inclusions, number fill, and packaging to give each set a clear identity.

For wholesale buyers, elemental themes make assortments easier to merchandise than random colors. Stores can group them by spell type, character concept, campaign environment, or gift collection, while brands can develop exclusive colorways and private label packaging.

Material options

Choose the material around price tier and visual effect.

  • Standard resin: practical for broad color ranges, readable sets, and starter wholesale assortments.
  • Sharp edge resin: supports layered colors, foil, botanical or fantasy inclusions, and premium shelf presentation.
  • Liquid core: adds moving visual effects suited to magic, storm, frost, poison, and arcane themes.
  • Metal dice: creates a heavier premium tier through plating, enamel color, engraving, and protective gift packaging.

Material selection affects MOQ, sample requirements, production process, packing protection, and target retail price. Compare wholesale resin dice, sharp edge resin dice, liquid core dice, and metal dice before setting the assortment.

B2B assortment planning

How to build an elemental dice collection for retail.

Start with core themes

Fire, frost, storm, and arcane provide clear color separation and broad fantasy appeal for an initial assortment.

Add dark themes

Poison, necrotic, and shadow extend the line for rogue, undead, curse, and monster-related campaigns.

Create price tiers

Mix standard resin, premium sharp edge or liquid core, and selected metal products instead of using one price level.

Plan replenishment

Use consistent theme names, SKU labels, display packing, and carton marks so stores can reorder successful designs.

Elemental dice FAQ

Questions from wholesale elemental dice buyers.

What are elemental dice sets?

Elemental dice sets are polyhedral RPG dice organized around fantasy forces such as fire, frost, storm, poison, radiant, necrotic, shadow, and arcane magic.

Can elemental dice be ordered wholesale?

Yes. Buyers can request individual themes, mixed retail assortments, sample kits, or private label collections. MOQ depends on material, SKU mix, packaging, and customization.

Which materials work for elemental RPG dice?

Elemental themes can be developed in standard resin, sharp edge resin, liquid core, metal, and specialty formats. Material affects visual effects, weight, finish, MOQ, packaging, and price.

Can I customize elemental dice colors and packaging?

Yes. Depending on the program, buyers can plan colors, transparency, inclusions, number fill, custom D20 faces, inserts, labels, boxes, and private label packaging.

Should I request samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Samples help confirm color, number readability, finish, weight, packaging fit, and how the collection looks together before wholesale production.
