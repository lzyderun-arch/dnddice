Material and price position

Choose the right dice material for your wholesale program.

Acrylic or plastic DND dice

Good for starter assortments, event kits, and price-sensitive shelves. Use when buyers need broad color coverage and simple dnd dice price planning.

Resin RPG dice

Better for premium color, transparent DND dice, glitter, inclusions, and themed collections. Resin works well for giftable 5e dice set programs.

Metal dice gaming sets

Best for heavier premium products, collector shelves, and display packaging. Metal dice usually need stronger boxes and sample review.

Liquid dice DND styles

Useful for high-impact product photos, premium launch drops, and sample kit presentations. Confirm bubble movement, clarity, and packaging before bulk orders.

What dice are in a standard 5e dice set?
