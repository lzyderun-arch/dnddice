MOQ and sample kit

Confirm D20 size, material, face detail, and packaging before bulk production.

Bulk D20 dice MOQ changes with material, size, color, custom face detail, packaging, and SKU count. A standard catalog D20 assortment is usually simpler than a custom logo D20 with new face artwork, premium box, barcode label, and event-specific packing.

Before bulk production, use a sample kit to check number readability, weight, size, color fill, finish, custom face clarity, and packaging fit. This is especially important for oversized D20 dice, metal D20 dice, dragon eye D20, and private label gift packaging. Buyers can review the sample kit options and custom packaging formats before finalizing the quote.

For a faster quote, send your buyer type, target quantity, D20 size, selected catalog references, logo or symbol file, packaging format, delivery country, launch date, and whether the D20 dice will be used for retail, events, Kickstarter rewards, or subscription boxes.

FAQ

Wholesale D20 dice questions.

Can I order D20 dice separately from full 7-piece sets?

Yes. Bulk D20 dice programs can focus on loose D20 assortments, oversized D20, premium collector D20, custom logo D20, or D20 gift packaging without requiring a full 7-piece dice set.

Can the 20 face be customized?

A buyer-owned logo or original symbol can be considered for the 20 face, depending on size, detail level, material, tooling method, and sample approval.

What D20 styles work best for retail?

Dragon eye D20, liquid core D20, sharp edge resin D20, oversized D20, metal D20, and themed colorways usually work well as impulse purchases, gifts, events, and campaign rewards.

What is the MOQ for bulk D20 dice?

MOQ depends on material, D20 size, color, custom face requirements, packaging, SKU count, and whether the buyer chooses catalog styles or new custom tooling.

Are bulk D20 dice useful for events and promotions?

Yes. Bulk D20 dice are practical for convention giveaways, store events, tournament prizes, RPG publisher promotions, Kickstarter add-ons, and subscription box inserts.

What should buyers send for a D20 quote?
