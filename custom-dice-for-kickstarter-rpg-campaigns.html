Planning point

When custom dice fit a crowdfunding campaign

Dice are strongest when they connect directly to the setting, class system, factions, monsters, magic schools, or campaign symbols. For crowdfunding, the dice should feel like part of the world rather than a generic accessory.

Planning point

Recommended dice types

Sharp edge resin works well for premium visual themes. Metal dice fit collector rewards and limited tiers. Oversized D20 dice can support launch events or deluxe bundles. Standard resin sets are practical for broader add-ons when cost and fulfillment weight matter.

Planning point

MOQ and sample planning

MOQ depends on material, selected SKUs, custom face artwork, packaging, and whether the campaign needs one design or a mixed assortment. Request samples before launch photos or before final bulk approval when color, number readability, and packaging presentation are important.

Planning point

Packaging for backer rewards

Common options include velvet bags, simple boxes, magnetic gift boxes, branded sleeves, insert cards, and carton packing by reward tier. Tell us whether the dice ship alone, inside a campaign box, or as an add-on SKU.

FAQ

Common buyer questions.

Can custom dice be used as Kickstarter stretch goals?

Yes. They work best when the theme, material, quantity target, packaging, and launch deadline are planned before the campaign promise is finalized.

Should creators order samples before the campaign launches?

If dice photos, color accuracy, packaging, or premium reward quality matter, samples are recommended before final campaign assets or before bulk production.

Can you copy official D&D symbols?

No. We can develop original fantasy RPG symbols, class-inspired concepts, dragon themes, elemental themes, and setting-specific artwork supplied by the creator.
