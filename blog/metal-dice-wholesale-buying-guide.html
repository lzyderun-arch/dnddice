Retailer Sourcing Guide

Metal dice wholesale buying guide for RPG retailers.

Metal dice are best used as premium RPG products: collector sets, gift boxes, convention table items, and higher-value game store shelf products. Retailers should compare theme, weight, finish, packaging, and sample quality before bulk ordering.

What makes metal dice different for wholesale buying?

Metal dice feel heavier, look more giftable, and usually command a higher retail price. That also means buyers need to pay more attention to packaging protection, number readability, surface finish, and shipping assumptions.

A metal dice assortment should not simply copy a resin dice assortment. It should focus on themes where metal adds value: dragon eye, skull, sword, gear, mechanical, alien, vine, and dragon pattern dice.

Best metal dice themes for RPG retailers.

Dragon eye dice work well for fantasy collectors. Gear and mechanical dice fit steampunk and sci-fi themes. Skull dice are useful for dark fantasy, Halloween, and necromancer collections. Sword dice can be merchandised for fighters, paladins, and battle-focused characters.

For a first order, pick fewer themes with stronger product names. Retail staff sell premium dice faster when the product story is easy to explain.

FAQ

Are metal dice good wholesale products for RPG retailers?

Yes. They work well as premium shelf products, gift sets, collector items, and convention booth products.

Do metal dice need better packaging?

Usually yes. Their weight and price point make tins, gift boxes, magnetic boxes, and protective inserts more important.

Should retailers sample metal dice first?

Yes. Samples help check finish, weight, number readability, packaging, and likely shelf price before bulk buying.
