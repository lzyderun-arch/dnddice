Sample Approval Guide

What should be included in a custom dice sample kit?

A useful custom dice sample kit should include representative materials, number fill examples, finish options, packaging samples, and clear notes for MOQ, production lead time, and quote requirements. The goal is to reduce bulk order risk before money is committed to production.

Sample kit checklist.

Sample itemWhat to checkWhy it matters
Resin dice samplesColor, number fill, clarity, inclusions, surface finishConfirms whether the product matches the target shelf price.
Sharp edge samplesPolish, edge finish, number readability, premium appearanceReduces risk for higher-value dice collections.
Metal dice samplesWeight, finish, number contrast, packaging protectionMetal dice need stronger buyer confidence before bulk orders.
Liquid core or specialty samplesMotion effect, clarity, consistency, display valueComplex dice should be reviewed before MOQ production.
Packaging samplesBag, tin, magnetic box, insert card, label, display cartonPackaging affects landed cost, perceived value, and shipping.

Do not judge samples only by appearance.

Good samples should answer commercial questions, not just visual questions. A buyer should ask whether the product can be reordered, whether packaging supports the retail price, whether the dice are easy to photograph, and whether the supplier can quote clearly.

If a buyer plans private label packaging, the sample stage should include box structure, insert direction, label format, and barcode or SKU requirements.

What to send when requesting samples.

  • Buyer type: game store, dice brand, distributor, Kickstarter creator, subscription box, or private label buyer.
  • Preferred materials: resin, sharp edge, metal, liquid core, hollow, oversized D20, or mixed.
  • Target quantity or MOQ range.
  • Packaging interest: bag, tin, gift box, display carton, insert card, or private label box.
  • Delivery country and target launch date.

FAQ

What should be included in a custom dice sample kit?

Representative dice materials, number fill examples, finish options, packaging samples, and MOQ or production notes.

Is a sample kit needed for every order?

It is most useful for new buyers, custom products, premium dice, private label packaging, and any product where visual finish or packaging matters.

Can samples include packaging?

Yes. Packaging samples can include bags, tins, gift boxes, inserts, labels, or display carton references.
