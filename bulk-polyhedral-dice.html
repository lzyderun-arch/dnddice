Build a bulk dice assortment around buyer behavior.

Bulk polyhedral dice work best when the assortment is not random. Game stores, event vendors, and distributors need a clear mix of entry price points, premium sets, giftable packaging, and fantasy themes that players can understand quickly.

MOQ and sample kits

Plan MOQ around SKU mix, not only total quantity.

Bulk dice MOQ depends on the number of SKUs, dice material, packaging type, private label requirements, and whether the order uses catalog styles or deeper customization. A buyer ordering 1,000 total sets across many SKUs may have a different plan from a buyer ordering 1,000 sets of one design.

Sample kits are useful before bulk DND dice orders because they let buyers check real weight, number readability, color fill, finish, box fit, and shelf presentation. For game stores, a sample kit can also help staff decide which fantasy themes are easiest to explain to customers.

When demand is uncertain, start with a focused catalog shortlist: a core 7-piece set, a D20 option, two to four theme families, one premium tier, and one packaging format. Expand the assortment after sell-through and reorder timing are clearer.
