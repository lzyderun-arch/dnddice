Custom workflow

From custom made dice set concept to wholesale-ready production.

  1. Brief and target market

    Share buyer type, country, theme direction, target retail price, expected order quantity, packaging requirement, and launch timeline.

  2. Theme and material proposal

    We map the concept into practical production options such as resin, sharp edge, liquid core, metal dice, oversized d20, number color, and packaging tier.

  3. Artwork and packaging plan

    Confirm logo placement, custom d20 face, number fill, insert card, box structure, display box layout, SKU naming, and carton organization. Review the custom packaging options before artwork is finalized.

  4. Sample production

    Physical samples verify color, finish, readability, feel, packaging fit, and shelf presentation before bulk commitment. The sample kit guide explains what buyers should check.

  5. Pre-production approval

    Approved sample photos, packaging files, quantity breakdown, defect tolerance, shipping method, and delivery schedule are confirmed before production.

  6. Bulk production and QC

    Production follows approved samples with checks for surface polish, number fill, edge quality, packaging accuracy, and mixed-SKU packing.

  7. Export packing and reorder support

    Orders can be packed by SKU, theme, display box, or customer carton mark to support distributors, game stores, and subscription boxes.

Custom dice FAQ

Common questions from custom DND dice buyers.

Can you make custom DND dice sets for wholesale buyers?

Yes. We support custom DND dice sets and 5e-compatible custom polyhedral dice for game stores, dice brands, distributors, and tabletop creators. Buyers can customize colors, inclusions, number fill, d20 faces, packaging, and private label details.

What can be customized on a custom RPG dice set?

A custom RPG dice set can include custom resin colors, transparent effects, glitter, inclusions, sharp edge finish, custom d20 symbols, logo engraving, number fill colors, custom metal dice finishes, insert cards, boxes, display cartons, and SKU labels.

Can I order custom metal dice or custom d20 dice sets?

Yes. Custom metal dice, custom metal dice sets, and custom d20 dice sets can be quoted based on finish, weight, engraving, symbol face, packaging, quantity, and sample approval requirements.

Can you make custom d20 dice with a logo or symbol?

Yes. Buyers can request custom d20 dice with a logo, symbol, icon, rune, faction mark, or simplified brand artwork on the high face. Vector artwork is recommended for clearer engraving, printing, and color fill.

Can custom DND dice include private label packaging?

Yes. Custom DND dice sets can be packed in bags, tins, magnetic boxes, retail boxes, display cartons, insert cards, barcode labels, and private label packaging depending on buyer channel, MOQ, and launch plan.

Do you offer custom gemstone dice or custom stone dice?

If you are comparing custom gemstone dice or custom stone dice, tell us whether you need real stone, gemstone-look resin, or a premium metal finish. Material choice affects MOQ, polishing, weight, breakage risk, packaging, and sample review.

Should I start with a sample kit or a full custom sample?

For a new buyer, a sample kit is useful for checking material quality, weight, number readability, packaging options, and finish level before developing a full custom sample. A full custom sample is better when the buyer already has artwork, color targets, packaging direction, and a realistic MOQ plan.

What should I send for a custom dice quote?

Send the dice type, target material, reference images, logo or custom d20 face, number color, estimated quantity, packaging format, buyer type, delivery country, launch date, and whether you need a sample kit before bulk production.

What is the MOQ for custom DND dice sets?

MOQ is quoted by material, number of colorways, custom face requirements, packaging, and production method. Catalog-based color and packaging programs can usually be planned differently from new molds, engraved faces, or specialty dice. See the wholesale dice MOQ guide for the main planning factors.

How long does a custom dice project take?

Timing depends on artwork readiness, material, sample revisions, packaging, order quantity, and shipping method. We provide a project-specific sample and production schedule after reviewing the complete brief.
